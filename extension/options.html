options & memory

Extension

Master switch: while off Blabla Meter touches no page (no scoring, badges, pickers or ad-blocking).

AI provider

Pick a provider, paste your key and press Save. Free tiers: Gemini, Groq, Mistral, OpenRouter; fully local: Ollama.

Jev instant score & ad-block — key and thresholds

TypeSafe is a separate paid scoring service. Its key powers both the instant Blabla score and the Jev ad-block decisions. Without it, the side panel offers the slower AI summary & highlights instead, and the 🚫 toggle still hides obvious ads. The key is sent with each scoring request; “Save key to bridge” also stores it in ~/.config/bbla/typesafe.key so ./run and the news ranker use it too.

Page access

By default Blabla Meter can read a page only after you click its toolbar icon on it. Allow all sites to analyze any tab straight from the side panel and to apply ad-blocking automatically on pages you have not clicked.

Learned preferences

Distilled from your thumbs, scores and notes every 5 feedback items. Sent with every analysis. Edit freely, one per line.

Bullshit categories

The highlighter uses these. The AI proposes new ones when a passage fits none (marked “learned”).

Blocked elements per site

    Learned score areas per site

    Sections you scored with the side panel's “◉ score” picker. They are included as blocks in every future scoring on that site.
